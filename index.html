How it works

Watch. Listen. Practise.

Dry official material, turned into something you will actually finish.

1

Watch

Every chapter is acted out in short animated lessons, a few minutes each. One idea at a time, in order, with nothing skipped.

2

Listen

Clear narration with captions that light up word by word, so you can follow on the bus, in the kitchen or with the sound off.

3

Practise

Questions after every part, timed mock tests like the real thing, flip cards for the dates and names that trip people up.
